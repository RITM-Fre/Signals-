About Signal

Signal is a privacy-first, offline-friendly toolbox for encoding, decoding, ciphering and hashing text and files. Every operation runs locally in your browser — nothing is sent to any server.

It was built to be a single place where you can quickly reach for the right tool, whether you're a developer debugging a Base64 string, a student learning classical ciphers, or someone who just wants to hash a password safely.

What you can do here

  • Encoders: Base64, Hex, Base32/58/85, URL encoding, HTML entities, Binary, Unicode escapes.
  • Classical ciphers: Caesar, ROT13, Vigenère, Atbash, Affine, Playfair.
  • Modern ciphers: XOR, AES, DES/3DES, ChaCha20 — all with key input.
  • Asymmetric cryptography: RSA, ECC and Diffie-Hellman keypair generation and operations.
  • Hashing: MD5, SHA-1, SHA-256, SHA-384, SHA-512, RIPEMD-160.
  • Password hashing: bcrypt, scrypt and Argon2id with salt support.
  • Extras: Morse code with Fingilish and Persian auto-transliteration, gzip compression, and image steganography.

Privacy

No data ever leaves your device. There are no analytics, no tracking scripts, and no network requests for any operation you perform. You can even use this page offline after the first load.
